GTA 5 Trophies & Achievements: all 78

78 entries · updated 2026-10-18

Tick them off as you go. Vinewood Vault tracks all 78 Trophies & Achievements on an offline map, alongside everything else you need for 100% in GTA 5. Free on iPhone and iPad.

Single Player - Platinum

  1. Los Santos Legend

    Awarded once every other trophy on this list has been collected.

Single Player - Gold

  1. Solid Gold, Baby!

    Collect a combined 70 Gold Medal ratings, mixing story missions with Strangers and Freaks jobs.

  2. Career Criminal

    Push your overall completion percentage up to 100%.

Single Player - Silver

  1. To Live or Die in Los Santos

    Finish the last main story mission - it doesn't matter which of the three endings you pick.

  2. San Andreas Sightseer

    Uncover the entire map - both Los Santos and Blaine County - along with most of the surrounding sea.

  3. Kifflom!

    Missable

    Missable - wrap it up before the finale. See Michael's full Epsilon Program storyline through to the end.

Single Player - Bronze

  1. Welcome to Los Santos

    Tied to the story - pops on its own once you finish the "Franklin and Lamar" mission.

  2. A Friendship Resurrected

    Story-related; fires automatically after you wrap up "Fame or Shame."

  3. A Fair Day's Pay

    Unlocks automatically once you've finished the "Caida Libre" mission.

  4. The Moment of Truth

    Pops on its own when you complete "Bury the Hatchet."

  5. Diamond Hard

    Unlocks automatically once "The Jewel Store Job" is finished.

  6. Subversive

    Story trophy that triggers automatically after "The Merryweather Heist."

  7. Blitzed

    Awarded automatically on completion of "Blitz Play."

  8. Small Town, Big Job

    Triggers automatically once "The Paleto Score" is completed.

  9. The Government Gimps

    Story-linked; pops automatically after finishing "The Bureau Raid."

  10. The Big One!

    Unlocks on its own when "The Big Score" is finished.

  11. All's Fare in Love and War

    Playing as Franklin, purchase the Downtown Cab Co. business and then finish one private fare.

  12. TP Industries Arms Race

    Missable

    Trevor only, so it is lost if you end the story with Something Sensible. Pick either other ending and Trevor lives, so you can finish it any time afterward. Have Trevor buy the McKenzie Field Hangar and clear all 10 gun-running missions.

  13. Multi-Disciplined

    Attain a gold medal in all applicable hobbies and pastimes.

    Just one Gold per hobby type, not every individual challenge: Shooting Range, Flight School, Off-Road Race, Sea Race, Street Race, and Triathlon - 6 total. Street Races unlock through, and are satisfied by, Hao's Strangers & Freaks mission "Shift Work."

  14. From Beyond the Stars

    Locate all 50 spaceship parts, then play the resulting mission, "The Final Frontier," as Franklin to hand them over to Omega.

  15. A Mystery, Solved

    Track down all 50 of Leonora Johnson's letter scraps, then play the resulting mission as Franklin to find out who killed her.

  16. Waste Management

    Buy the abandoned dock and gather up every barrel of nuclear waste.

  17. Red Mist

    Missable

    Trevor only, so it is lost if you end the story with Something Sensible. With either other ending Trevor lives and you can finish this whenever. Clear all 5 of Trevor's Rampage challenges.

  18. Show Off

    Clear every Stunt Jump scattered around the map.

  19. Three Man Army

    Missable

    Needs all three protagonists alive and switchable, so it is off the table after Something Sensible or The Time's Come. Keep everyone with The Third Way and you can do it any time after the story. Gather Michael, Franklin, and Trevor together off-mission and hold a 3-star wanted level for 3 minutes.

  20. Out of Your Depth

    Let a shark take you down while you're out in open water.

  21. Altruist Acolyte

    Missable

    Trevor only, so ending the story with Something Sensible closes it off; either other ending keeps him. Playing as Trevor, pick up a hitchhiker from a random encounter and drop them at the Altruist Cult instead of their real destination.

  22. A Lot of Cheddar

    Between Michael, Franklin, and Trevor, spend $200 million total.

    The fastest legitimate route to $200 million is Lester's five assassination contracts - each one has a stock play attached (something to buy beforehand, something to sell into afterward, sometimes a second follow-up trade). Best tackled after the main story's done, once all three characters have full bankrolls to invest. Each mission below has its own specific stock and timing.

  23. Trading Pure Alpha

    Make a profitable trade on the in-game stock market.

  24. Pimp My Sidearm

    Max out every mod slot on a single weapon at Ammu-Nation.

  25. Wanted: Alive or Alive

    Missable

    Trevor only, so Something Sensible rules it out. Maude also hands out only a limited run of bail-bond targets, so don't leave every one until last. Playing as Trevor, bring a target in alive and hand them to Maude.

  26. Los Santos Customs

    Fully customize a vehicle at any Los Santos Customs shop.

  27. Close Shave

    Finish off every Under the Bridge marker and every Knife Flight challenge.

  28. A New Perspective

    Log 15 cumulative hours playing in first-person view. Exclusive to the PS4/current-gen re-release - not available on PS3/360.

Online - Gold

  1. Above the Law

    GTA Online: climb all the way to Rank 100.

Online - Silver

  1. Making Moves

    GTA Online: climb to Rank 50.

  2. Decorated

    GTA Online: collect 30 Platinum Awards.

    Live-linked to the GTA Online Awards checklist: this ticks itself off once 30 of those Awards are marked Platinum.

Online - Bronze

  1. Off the Plane

    GTA Online: get through the opening character-creation intro.

  2. Three-Bit Gangster

    GTA Online: climb to Rank 25.

  3. Numero Uno

    GTA Online: take first place at least once in each of the 15 competitive modes.

    GTA Online trophy - you need a 1st-place finish in each of the 15 competitive game types below at least once. Winning in a Closed Friends Session against one other person (or the minimum player count each mode needs) is the fastest path; just make sure nobody quits mid-match or it won't count.

    Objectives

    • Deathmatch
    • Team Deathmatch
    • Land Race
    • Air Race
    • Sea Race
    • GTA Race
    • Rally Race
    • Arm Wrestling
    • Darts
    • Golf
    • Tennis
    • Shooting Range
    • Survival
    • Parachuting
    • Gang Attack

  4. The Midnight Club

    GTA Online: win 5 races while driving a vehicle you've personally customized.

  5. Unnatural Selection

    GTA Online: make it through all 10 waves of a Survival match.

  6. Backseat Driver

    GTA Online: acting as co-driver in Rally Mode, guide your driver to a 1st-place finish.

  7. Run Like The Wind

    GTA Online: stay alive for a full in-game day while carrying a bounty on your head.

  8. Clean Sweep

    GTA Online: clear a Gang Attack without dying, racking up at least 10 kills along the way.

  9. Stick Up Kid

    GTA Online: rob every one of the 20 robbable stores.

  10. Enjoy Your Stay

    GTA Online: sample the city's core activities - strip club, clothing store, barber, tattoo parlor, arm wrestling, a store robbery, darts, golf, tennis, and the shooting range.

    Online trophy - doing these in Story Mode doesn't count, even though most have a direct Story Mode equivalent. Each one has to happen in a GTA Online session specifically, and just taking part is enough - no need to win.

    Objectives

    • Strip Club
    • Clothing Store
    • Barber
    • Tattoo Parlor
    • Arm Wrestling
    • Store Robbery
    • Darts
    • Golf
    • Tennis
    • Shooting Range

  11. Crew Cut

    GTA Online: run a Job together with a fellow member of your Crew.

  12. Full Refund

    GTA Online: hunt down the mugger who robbed you, kill them, and take your cash back.

  13. Dialling Digits

    GTA Online: call in a Merryweather backup chopper for the first time.

  14. American Dream

    GTA Online: hold ownership of an apartment, a garage, and an insured vehicle all at the same time.

Online - Heists DLC - Silver

  1. Mastermind

    GTA Online, Heists Update: earn a combined 25 Platinum medals across Heist Setup and Finale missions.

  2. Live a Little

    GTA Online, Heists Update: spend a combined $8,000,000 on vehicles introduced with the Heist Update.

Online - Heists DLC - Bronze

  1. Be Prepared

    GTA Online: complete a Heist Setup mission.

  2. Shot Caller

    GTA Online: as Heist Leader, put up the cash to set up a Heist for the first time.

  3. Four Way

    GTA Online: as Heist Leader, split a Finale's take evenly - 25% to all 4 crew members.

  4. Can't Touch This

    GTA Online: complete a Heist Finale without taking any damage.

  5. In the Name of Science

    GTA Online: complete both The Humane Labs Raid and Series A Funding, as Leader or Crew.

  6. Dead Presidents

    GTA Online: complete both The Fleeca Job and The Pacific Standard Job, as Leader or Crew.

  7. Parole Day

    GTA Online: complete The Prison Break, as Leader or Crew.

Single Player - Director Mode DLC - Silver

  1. Majestic

    GTA Online: create and export 10 videos using the Rockstar Editor - or, since the Editor was pulled from PS4/Xbox One in Feb. 2024, enter Director Mode 10 times in the same session instead.

  2. Ensemble Piece

    GTA Online: unlock every Story Character, then enter Director Mode playing as one of them.

  3. Cryptozoologist

    Unlock every animal actor available for Director Mode.

    Only the land and air Peyote Plants count - eating one of the 5 underwater ones turns you into a sea creature, and Director Mode has no actor for those, so they don't contribute here.

Single Player - Director Mode DLC - Bronze

  1. Vinewood Visionary

    GTA Online: create and export a video using the Rockstar Editor - or enter Director Mode 5 times in the same session.

  2. First Time Director

    GTA Online: enter Director Mode for the first time, playing as any unlocked actor.

  3. Method Actor

    GTA Online: enter Director Mode playing as one of your own GTA Online characters.

  4. Humans of Los Santos

    GTA Online: unlock every Special Character, then enter Director Mode playing as one of them.

  5. Animal Lover

    GTA Online: enter Director Mode playing as an unlocked animal actor, for the first time.

  6. Cult Movie

    GTA Online: enter Director Mode playing as Cris Formage.

  7. Location Scout

    GTA Online: visit every one of the 27 starting locations available in Director Mode.

Online - Doomsday Heist DLC - Silver

  1. The Bogdan Problem

    GTA Online: complete the Act II Finale of The Doomsday Heist, as Leader or Crew.

  2. The Doomsday Scenario

    GTA Online: complete the Act III Finale of The Doomsday Heist, as Leader or Crew.

  3. A World Worth Saving

    GTA Online: complete the Finales of all 3 Acts of The Doomsday Heist, as Leader or Crew.

  4. Elitist

    GTA Online: clear all 3 Elite Challenges across The Doomsday Heist's Acts - one per Act, each with its own time limit and no-deaths requirement.

  5. Masterminds

    GTA Online: clear the Criminal Mastermind challenge on The Doomsday Heist with a 2-player, a 3-player, and a 4-player crew - same crew throughout each run, hard difficulty, no deaths.

Online - Doomsday Heist DLC - Bronze

  1. Getting Started

    GTA Online: set up The Doomsday Heist - pay the $65,000 setup cost from your Facility's Planning Room.

  2. The Data Breaches

    GTA Online: complete the Act I Finale of The Doomsday Heist, as Leader or Crew.

  3. Orbital Obliteration

    GTA Online: kill another player using your Facility's Orbital Cannon.
