GTA 5 Epsilon Tracts: all 10 locations

10 entries · updated 2026-10-18

10 tracts hidden across San Andreas, unlocked one at a time after finishing the Epsilon Program missions. Bonus content - not required for 100%.

Tick them off as you go. Vinewood Vault tracks all 10 Epsilon Tracts on an offline map, alongside everything else you need for 100% in GTA 5. Free on iPhone and iPad.

  1. 1Epsilon Tract 1

    Mount Chiliad

    On the observation deck at the summit of Mount Chiliad, the first tract, available once the Epsilon Program missions are finished.

    Watch the video

  2. 2Epsilon Tract 2

    Palomino Highlands

    On the ocean floor in the bay off the east coast, in front of an old sunken paddle steamer.

    Watch the video

  3. 3Epsilon Tract 3

    Pacific Ocean

    Atop the northernmost island in the Pacific Ocean, north of Paleto Bay.

    Watch the video

  4. 4Epsilon Tract 4

    Chiliad Mountain State Wilderness

    In a doorway nook near the middle of the road tunnel that runs through Mount Chiliad.

    Watch the video

  5. 5Epsilon Tract 5

    Little Seoul

    At the large, old church on the corner of San Andreas Avenue and Ginger Street.

    Watch the video

  6. 6Epsilon Tract 6

    Paleto Cove

    On the low dock at the Sonar Collections Dock, near the Dinghy and Submersible.

    Watch the video

  7. 7Epsilon Tract 7

    Richman

    Next to the entrance of a large, fancy mansion in the northwestern part of Richman.

    Watch the video

  8. 8Epsilon Tract 8

    Mount Chiliad

    Behind the summit telescope platform, at the base of the tallest tree in San Andreas.

    Watch the video

  9. 9Epsilon Tract 9

    Downtown Los Santos

    On the roof of the Maze Bank Tower, the tallest building in Los Santos. Bring a helicopter.
