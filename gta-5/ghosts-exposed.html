GTA Online Ghosts Exposed: all 10 locations

10 entries · updated 2026-10-18

GTA Online Halloween event: 10 ghosts to photograph with your phone camera, one per in-game hour between 8pm and 6am, each there for about 2 real minutes. GTA$20,000 and 500 RP each, plus GTA$50,000 and the Ghosts Exposed Jacket for all 10. The 10th, Devin Weston, only appears once you've photographed the other nine. Seasonal, back each October.

Tick them off as you go. Vinewood Vault tracks all 10 Ghosts Exposed on an offline map, alongside everything else you need for 100% in GTA Online. Free on iPhone and iPad.
