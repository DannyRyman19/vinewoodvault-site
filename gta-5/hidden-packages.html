GTA 5 Hidden Packages: all 15

15 entries · updated 2026-10-18

15 cash-filled briefcases - 11 sunk in the ocean, 4 waiting at the Altruist Camp after the cult shootout. Bonus content - not required for 100%.

Tick them off as you go. Vinewood Vault tracks all 15 Hidden Packages on an offline map, alongside everything else you need for 100% in GTA 5. Free on iPhone and iPad.

  1. Hidden Package 1

    Paleto Cove · Reward: $12,000

    Underwater near the tail section of a wrecked FlyUS airplane.

  2. Hidden Package 2

    North Chumash · Reward: $12,500

    Underwater, inside a detached aircraft cabin.

  3. Hidden Package 3

    Los Santos Docks · Reward: $11,000

    Underwater by a tanker ship wreckage.

  4. Hidden Package 4

    Fort Zancudo · Reward: $9,000

    On a wrecked ship near the base.

  5. Hidden Package 5

    Pacific Bluffs · Reward: $25,000

    Atop a sunken submarine in deep water.

  6. Hidden Package 6

    Banning · Reward: $7,500

    Sitting on a shipping container in shallow water - no scuba gear needed.

  7. Hidden Package 7

    Murrieta Heights · Reward: $16,500

    Underwater, tucked beneath the right tail wing of a crashed plane.

  8. Hidden Package 8

    Palomino Highlands · Reward: $7,000

    Underwater in a steamer ship wreck near El Burro Heights.

  9. Hidden Package 9

    Tataviam Mountains · Reward: $20,000

    Inside a destroyed ship near the N.O.O.S.E. headquarters.

  10. Hidden Package 10

    RON Alternates Wind Farm · Reward: $10,000

    Inside a crashed military truck container in deep water.

  11. Hidden Package 11

    Paleto Bay · Reward: $8,000

    Underwater near a rusted crop duster, in shark-infested waters.

  12. Hidden Package 12

    Altruist Camp · Reward: $25,000

    First of four briefcases inside the compound cabins, unlocked after the Altruist cult shootout.

  13. Hidden Package 13

    Altruist Camp · Reward: $25,000

    Second of four briefcases inside the compound cabins.

  14. Hidden Package 14

    Altruist Camp · Reward: $25,000

    Third of four briefcases inside the compound cabins.

  15. Hidden Package 15
