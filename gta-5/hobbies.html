  4. 22Freeway

    La Mesa · Given by: Hao · After: Airport · Cost: $1,250 · Reward: $5,625

    Two laps weaving on and off the Olympic and Del Perro freeway ramps.

  5. 23Vespucci Canals

    Vespucci, Del Perro · Given by: Hao · After: Freeway · Cost: $1,500 · Reward: $6,750

    Bikes only for this two-lap course, launching from Invention Court and threading Vespucci and Del Perro.

  6. 29Eco Friendly

    RON Alternates Wind Farm · Given by: Ron Jakowski · After: Mr. Philips · Reward: $500 · Weapons: Off-road truck
