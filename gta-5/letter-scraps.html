GTA 5 Letter Scraps: all 50 locations

50 entries · updated 2026-10-20

50 scraps of Beverly's stalker letters, all required for 100%. They appear after the mission Repossession, and any of the three protagonists can pick them up. Collecting all 50 unlocks the mission A Starlet in Vinewood for Franklin.

Tick them off as you go. Vinewood Vault tracks all 50 Letter Scraps on an offline map, alongside everything else you need for 100% in GTA 5. Free on iPhone and iPad.
