GTA 5 Miscellaneous 100% Tasks: all 11

11 entries · updated 2026-10-14

Tick them off as you go. Vinewood Vault tracks all 11 Miscellaneous on an offline map, alongside everything else you need for 100% in GTA 5. Free on iPhone and iPad.

  1. Purchase a Vehicle Online

    Order any single vehicle through one of the in-game car websites, accessed via a phone or in-world computer.

  2. Walk Chop

    Head out with Chop, Franklin's dog, for a stroll through the neighborhood.

  3. Play Fetch with Chop

    Toss a ball around the yard with Chop and let him fetch it.

  4. Booty Call

    Distinct from just paying for a dance (that's the Hobbies & Pastimes requirement) - rack up repeat private dances from the same Vanilla Unicorn dancer until she's added as a phone contact, then call her and follow through on the Booty Call itself.

  5. Hire a Prostitute

    Pull over for a streetwalker and pay for her company.

  6. Rob a Store

    Point a gun at a clerk in any of the 19 robbable corner shops or liquor stores and empty the till.

  7. Watch a Movie

    Catch a showing at any of the movie theaters scattered around Vinewood.

Friend Activities

  1. Friend Activity: Cinema

    Ring up a friend and catch a film together at the theater.

  2. Friend Activity: Darts

    Meet up with a friend for a round of darts.

  3. Friend Activity: Strip Club

    Take a friend along on a night out at the Vanilla Unicorn.

  4. Friend Activity: Bar

    Grab drinks with a friend at a local bar.
