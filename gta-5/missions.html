GTA 5 guide

GTA 5 missions and gold medals

77 entries · updated 2026-10-20

All 69 story missions, in order. Gold medal every mission for the platinum-level challenge, or just play through for the 100% stat. ★ marks missions on the easy path to your 70 Gold Medals for Solid Gold, Baby! - the rest are still doable, just tougher, so skip them if you've already got your 70.

Tick them off as you go. Vinewood Vault tracks all 77 Story Missions on an offline map, alongside everything else you need for 100% in GTA 5. Free on iPhone and iPad.

  1. 1Prologue

    Nine years before the main story, a bank robbery in the snowy town of Ludendorff falls apart while playing younger versions of Michael, Trevor, and partner Brad.

    There's no Gold Medal target for this one - simply finishing the mission satisfies the Bronze tier.

  2. 2Franklin and Lamar

    Franklin & Lamar · Given by: Simeon

    In Franklin's opening mission, he and Lamar reclaim a customer's overdue vehicle on behalf of Simeon's used-car dealership.

    Gold medal objectives

    • Deliver the repo car with minimal damage (Not a Scratch)
    • Use Franklin's special ability for 7 seconds (Focused Driver)
    • Win the race against Lamar (Winner)
    • Avoid hitting any aliens in the movie studio (We Come in Peace)

  3. 3Repossession

    Franklin & Lamar · Given by: Simeon

    Simeon sends Franklin and Lamar to repossess an overdue motorcycle in Vespucci Beach, and the job turns into a shootout with Vagos gang members guarding it.

    Gold medal objectives

    • Complete within 6:30
    • Shoot the gasoline trail to ignite it
    • Get 6 headshot kills
    • Finish with at least 70% shooting accuracy

  36. 36Hotel Assassination

    Blitz Play & Assassinations · Given by: Lester

    Lester sends Franklin to eliminate a mark at a hotel as part of his stock-market manipulation scheme.

    Before you take this contract, sink every dollar across all three characters into Betta Pharmaceuticals on BAWSAQ. Once the target's down, keep sleeping/saving to track the price and cash out around an 80% gain, then roll the proceeds into Bilkington on LCN - give that one roughly a week to climb before selling again.

    Gold medal objectives

    • Kill the target using a sniper rifle (Sniper Kill Bonus)
    • Time Taken is tracked but appears to auto-fulfill regardless of duration
    • Money Earned from the hit cannot fail as long as the mission is completed

  39. 39The Multi Target Assassination

    Blitz Play & Assassinations · Given by: Lester

    For another of Lester's market-rigging contracts, Franklin has to take down several targets in a single outing.

    Load every character's cash into Debonaire on LCN before starting this one. It tends to peak around an 80% return after the mission - sell out completely, then put everything into Redwood on the same exchange. Redwood runs much hotter, often clearing 300% before it tops out, so it's worth the extra few in-game days to wait.

    Gold medal objectives

    • Kill all targets as quickly as possible (Speedy Killer Bonus)
    • Money Earned equals base reward ($5000) plus speed bonus

  40. 40The Vice Assassination

    Blitz Play & Assassinations · Given by: Lester

    Franklin kills an executive connected to a rival company as part of Lester's stock manipulation plot.

    Buy into Fruit on BAWSAQ ahead of this contract; it usually climbs about 50% once the job's done. Sell there and shift the proceeds into Facade, also on BAWSAQ, for a smaller but still worthwhile follow-up gain before it levels off.

    Gold medal objectives

    • Earn the full $3,000 money bonus from the hit

  41. 41The Bus Assassination

    Blitz Play & Assassinations · Given by: Lester

    One of Lester's contracts has Franklin taking out a target while riding along on a moving city bus.

    This one runs backwards from the rest - don't invest beforehand. Once the mission wraps, put your money into Vapid on BAWSAQ and give it a couple of in-game days; it typically runs close to double before the price peaks.

    Gold medal objectives

    • Earn the $5,000 base payout
    • Kill the target using the bus (Hit and Run bonus)
