GTA Online Movie Props: all 10

10 entries · updated 2026-10-14

GTA Online: 10 movie props hidden across Los Santos and Blaine County for Solomon Richards, added with the Los Santos Summer Special. Collecting all 10 unlocks the Space Interloper Outfit.

Tick them off as you go. Vinewood Vault tracks all 10 Movie Props on an offline map, alongside everything else you need for 100% in GTA Online. Free on iPhone and iPad.
