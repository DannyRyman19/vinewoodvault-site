GTA 5 Nuclear Waste: all 30 locations

30 entries · updated 2026-10-20

30 barrels of nuclear waste on the seabed. You collect them with the submarine that comes with the Sonar Collections Dock, a property that unlocks for $250,000 after The Merryweather Heist. Bonus content, not required for 100%. The dock also gives you a phone app in game that marks the barrels you still need, so cross-check it against these locations as you dive.

Tick them off as you go. Vinewood Vault tracks all 30 Nuclear Waste on an offline map, alongside everything else you need for 100% in GTA 5. Free on iPhone and iPad.
