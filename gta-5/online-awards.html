GTA Online guide

GTA Online Awards: all 446

446 entries · updated 2026-10-20

GTA Online career Awards from Pause Menu > Stats > Awards. Rockstar rates most Bronze/Silver/Gold/Platinum on repeated performance; the "one-time Platinum" ones jump straight to Platinum the first time you clear them and are starred here. Earning 30 Platinum Awards unlocks the Decorated trophy.

Tick them off as you go. Vinewood Vault tracks all 446 GTA Online Awards on an offline map, alongside everything else you need for 100% in GTA Online. Free on iPhone and iPad.
