GTA Online Peyote Plants: all 76 locations

76 entries · updated 2026-10-18

GTA Online: 76 peyote plants across San Andreas - eat one to roam as an animal for a while. A separate set from the single-player peyote plants. A golden peyote near Mount Chiliad, available once the other 76 are done, turns you into the Sasquatch.

Tick them off as you go. Vinewood Vault tracks all 76 Peyote Plants (Online) on an offline map, alongside everything else you need for 100% in GTA Online. Free on iPhone and iPad.
