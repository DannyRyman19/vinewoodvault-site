GTA 5 Peyote Plants: all 27 locations

27 entries · updated 2026-10-20

27 peyote cacti scattered across San Andreas - eat one to transform into an animal for a while. Bonus content - not required for 100%. If a plant is missing when you get there, a quick save and reload usually makes it appear.

Tick them off as you go. Vinewood Vault tracks all 27 Peyote Plants on an offline map, alongside everything else you need for 100% in GTA 5. Free on iPhone and iPad.
