GTA 5 Properties: all 23 locations

23 entries · updated 2026-10-14

Income-generating businesses and vehicle storage. 5 income-generating properties are required for 100%.

Tick them off as you go. Vinewood Vault tracks all 23 Properties on an offline map, alongside everything else you need for 100% in GTA 5. Free on iPhone and iPad.

Businesses

  1. 1LSPD Auto Impound

    Davis, Los Santos · Cost: $150,000

    Franklin runs this impound lot as a towing operation, earning $500 for every vehicle he brings in. Opens up once Tonya Wiggins' side jobs are finished.

  2. 2Downtown Cab Co.

    East Vinewood, Los Santos · Cost: $200,000

    Franklin-owned taxi firm that pays out $2,000 weekly and lets its owner hail cabs anywhere for free.

  3. 3McKenzie Field Hangar

    Grapeseed, Blaine County · Cost: $150,000

    Opens up a string of Trevor-only gun-running jobs, both by land and by air, each paying somewhere between $5,000 and $7,000.

  4. 4Sonar Collections Dock

    Paleto Cove, Blaine County · Cost: $250,000

    Base for diving up radioactive waste barrels scattered around the map; $23,000 lands per barrel recovered, with a $250,000 bonus for salvaging the full set of 11. Available once The Merryweather Heist wraps.

  5. 5Los Santos Customs

    Grand Senora Desert, Blaine County · Cost: $349,000

    Weekly earnings of $1,600 from this customs branch, and owning it lets Franklin modify his cars here without paying a dime.

  6. 6Doppler Cinema

    Downtown Vinewood, Los Santos · Cost: $10,000,000

    Michael's cinema investment, bringing in $132,200 every week.

  7. 7Ten Cent Theater

    Textile City, Los Santos · Cost: $20,000,000

    A Michael-owned picture house that generates $264,000 in weekly revenue.

  8. 8Tivoli Cinema

    Morningwood, Los Santos · Cost: $30,000,000

    Another cinema on Michael's books, worth $142,300 per week.

  9. 9Los Santos Golf Club

    Los Santos · Cost: $150,000,000

    This golf course pays its owner $264,500 weekly and waives green fees entirely, letting them tee off in whatever outfit they're wearing.

  10. 10Car Scrapyard

    El Burro Heights, Los Santos · Cost: $275,000

    Crush cars here for $150 a pop, regardless of which protagonist owns the yard.

  11. 11Smoke on the Water

    La Puerta, Los Santos · Cost: $204,000

    Franklin's seafood restaurant, clearing $9,300 a week.

  12. 12Tequi-la-la

    West Vinewood, Los Santos · Cost: $2,000,000

    Nightclub earning $16,500 weekly for whichever protagonist buys it; the purchase only opens up after finishing the Vinewood Souvenirs mission involving Willy.

  13. 13Pitchers

    Downtown Vinewood, Los Santos · Cost: $750,000

    This dive bar brings in $7,100 a week no matter which of the three owns it.

  14. 14The Hen House

    Paleto Bay, Blaine County · Cost: $80,000

    Modest corner store earning $920 weekly, purchasable by any of the three leads.

  15. 15Hookies

    North Chumash, Blaine County · Cost: $600,000

    Bar generating $4,700 a week, available for purchase by either Franklin or Michael.

  16. 16Vanilla Unicorn

    Strawberry, Los Santos · Cost: Free (complete Hang Ten)

    Strip club paying $5,000 weekly that also serves as one of Trevor's safehouses, with ownership unlocking complimentary private dances.

Vehicle Storage

  1. 17Puerto Del Sol Marina

    Puerto Del Sol, Los Santos · Cost: $75,000

    A dock where any of the three protagonists can keep their boats.

  2. 18Vespucci Helipad

    Puerto Del Sol, Los Santos · Cost: $419,850

    Helipad for storing choppers, open to purchase by Franklin or Michael.

  3. 19Los Santos International Hangar

    Los Santos International Airport, Los Santos · Cost: $1,378,600

    Hangar for parking planes that also grants runway access at LSIA; buyable by Franklin or Michael.

  4. 20Sandy Shores Airfield

    Sandy Shores, Blaine County · Cost: Free (complete Nervous Ron)

    Trevor's airfield for stashing both planes and helicopters, handed over free of charge once Nervous Ron is completed.

  5. 21Grove Street Garage

    Davis, Los Santos · Cost: $30,000

    Franklin's garage holds up to four vehicles and comes with access to the Special Vehicles menu.

  6. 22Pillbox Hill Garage

    Pillbox Hill, Los Santos · Cost: $30,000

    Four-car garage for Trevor that also unlocks the Special Vehicles menu.

  7. 23Vinewood Garage

    East Vinewood, Los Santos · Cost: $30,000

    Michael's four-car garage, which likewise opens up the Special Vehicles menu.
