GTA 5 Random Events: all 57

57 entries · updated 2026-10-14

Events that trigger while traversing the map. Only 14 of 57 are required for 100%.

Tick them off as you go. Vinewood Vault tracks all 57 Random Events on an offline map, alongside everything else you need for 100% in GTA 5. Free on iPhone and iPad.

  1. Abandoned Vehicles 1

    Smoke Tree Road, Grand Senora Desert · After: Pulling Favors

    Approach a parked Surfer van and a prankster ambushes you, stripping you to your underwear and dumping you on the railway tracks.

  2. Abandoned Vehicles 2

    RON Alternates Wind Farm · After: Pulling Favors

    A Journey van sitting by itself conceals two rednecks who attack if you get too close.

  3. Arrests 1

    Grapeseed, near O'Neil's farm · After: Pulling Favors

    Side with an officer chasing down a runaway suspect, or take out the officer and let the suspect go free.

  4. Arrests 2

    Senora Freeway, RON Alternates Wind Farm · After: Pulling Favors

    Assist a park ranger in bringing down an escaping criminal, or gun down the ranger and free the criminal.

  5. ATM Robberies

    Various ATMs across San Andreas · After: Pulling Favors

    Run down a mugger snatching cash from someone at an ATM, then pocket the money or hand it back. This one keeps recurring, though completing it a single time is all that counts.

  6. Bike Thief City 1

    Little Bighorn Avenue, Rancho · After: Pulling Favors

    Track down a bike thief and bring the bicycle back to owner Gray Nicholson, earning a stamina increase plus a stock market tip.

  7. Bike Thief City 2

    Bridge Street, Hawick · After: Pulling Favors

    Catch a bicycle thief and hand the bike back to its owner for a boost to stamina.

  8. Border Patrols 1

    Mount Haan Road, Grand Senora Desert · After: Pulling Favors

    A gang of bikers jumps you, assuming you're an outsider - fight them off.

  9. Border Patrols 2

    Tataviam Mountains · After: Pulling Favors

    Motorcycle-riding rednecks mistake you for an immigrant and attack - take them down.

  10. Border Patrols 3

    Raton Canyon · After: Pulling Favors

    A pack of bikers ambushes you here too, wrongly pegging you as a foreigner - deal with them.

  11. Burial

    North Point, Paleto Bay · After: Pulling Favors

    Stop two men in the middle of burying a woman alive, then get her somewhere safe.

  12. Bus Tour

    Alta Street, Downtown Vinewood · After: Pulling Favors

    Hop aboard a sightseeing bus through Vinewood and stay on until the route wraps up.

  13. Car Theft 1

    Great Ocean Highway, North Chumash · After: Pulling Favors

    Pursue a carjacker who's made off with a woman's pink Peyote, and either give it back or drive off with it.

  14. Car Theft 2

    Exceptionalists Way, Los Santos International Airport · After: Pulling Favors

    Chase a thief fleeing with a woman's Rocoto and choose to return the car or keep it for yourself.

  15. Chase Thieves City 1

    Hawick Avenue, Hawick · After: Pulling Favors

    Take down Lost bikers mid-robbery at a store and decide whether to keep or return the cash.

  16. Chase Thieves City 2

    San Andreas Avenue, Textile City · After: Pulling Favors

    Gun down Lost members after they snatch someone's wallet, then choose to keep the money or give it back.

  17. Chase Thieves Country 1

    Grapeseed Main Street, Grapeseed · After: Pulling Favors

    Stop a wallet theft by Lost bikers out here as well, then keep or hand back the money.

  18. Chase Thieves Country 2

    Route 68, Grand Senora Desert · After: Pulling Favors

    Take out Lost gang members robbing a pedestrian outside the Checkout! store, then decide the cash's fate.

  19. Crash Rescue

    Great Ocean Highway, Mount Chiliad · After: The Jewel Store Job

    Rush Taliana Martinez, the only one left alive after a heist went sideways, to safety before she bleeds out - saving her adds her to your crew roster.

  20. Construction Accident

    Calais Avenue, Little Seoul · After: Pulling Favors

    Operate a Dozer to shift pipes and free a worker trapped in a Utility Truck that's caught fire.

  21. Countryside Gang Fight

    Marina Drive, Alamo Sea · After: Pulling Favors

    Free a hostage being threatened by Lost bikers, then hold off a second group that rolls in on motorcycles. Only available as Michael or Franklin.

  22. Countryside Robbery

    Route 68, Harmony · After: Pulling Favors

    Come across a firefight between police and robbers - grab the briefcases left behind or aid the robbers' getaway.

  23. Deal Gone Wrong

    Chiliad Mountain State Wilderness · After: Pulling Favors

    A drug deal has ended in a shootout - take the briefcase from the dying gangsters, then defend it from a gang sent to get it back.

  24. Domestic

    Didion Drive, Vinewood Hills · After: Pulling Favors

    Offer a ride to a man his wife just threw out, which unlocks Castro Lagano as a tough golf rival.

  25. Drug Shootout

    Mount Chiliad, Blaine County · After: Pulling Favors

    Clear out the gunmen defending a small farm and take the briefcase stashed in a nearby shed. Trevor-exclusive, and it only happens once.

  26. Drunk Drivers 1

    Aguja Street, La Puerta · After: Pulling Favors

    Chauffeur an intoxicated man safely to his home on Alta Place in Hawick.

  27. Drunk Drivers 2

    Armadillo Avenue, Sandy Shores · After: Pulling Favors

    Get a tipsy couple back to their motel room in one piece.

  28. Escape Paparazzi

    Vinewood Boulevard, Downtown Vinewood · After: Pulling Favors

    Shake off a horde of paparazzi chasing celebrity Lacey Jonas and get her home safely.

  29. Gang Intimidation

    El Rancho Boulevard, El Burro Heights · After: Pulling Favors

    A woman pleads for help, but it's a setup - follow her in and take out the armed crooks waiting to jump you. Michael or Trevor only.

  30. Getaway Driver

    Strawberry Avenue and Forum Drive, Strawberry · After: Pulling Favors

    Patrick McReary and his partner need a getaway after robbing a drugstore - help them flee or gun them down instead.

  31. Hitch Lift 1

    Ineseno Road, Banham Canyon · After: Pulling Favors

    Get a broken-down motorist where he's headed inside two game hours to receive a Tinkle stock tip.

  32. Hitch Lift 2

    North Calafia Way, Mount Chiliad · After: Pulling Favors

    Drop Ursula off at home to unlock her as a Booty Call contact. Only Franklin and Trevor can trigger this.

  33. Hitch Lift 3

    East Joshua Road, San Chianski Mountain Range · After: Pulling Favors

    Take a hitchhiker home, then fend off her jealous boyfriend when he shows up swinging.

  34. Hitch Lift 4

    Route 68, Great Chaparral · After: Pulling Favors

    Escort a bride who's fled the altar home, while her groom chases you down.

  35. Luring Girl Into Alley

    Supply Street, La Mesa · After: Pulling Favors

    A girl asks for assistance and leads you into a trap - eliminate her and her partner.

  36. Mugging 1

    Chamberlain Hills, Strawberry Avenue · After: Pulling Favors

    Run down a pickpocket who's grabbed a pedestrian's wallet, then either keep the money or give it back.

  37. Mugging 2

    Meteor Street, Hawick · After: Pulling Favors

    Intervene in an alley attack, taking out the assailant before he kills the woman.

  38. Mugging 3

    Vespucci Boulevard, Pillbox Hill · After: Pulling Favors

    Pursue a wallet thief and decide whether to return the stolen cash or keep it.

  39. Prisoner Lifts 1

    Grand Senora Desert, north of Bolingbroke Penitentiary · After: Pulling Favors

    Get a runaway prisoner clear of the police tailing him. Only playable as Michael or Franklin.

  40. Prisoner Lifts 2

    Grand Senora Desert, south of Bolingbroke Penitentiary · After: Pulling Favors

    A prisoner asks for a ride, then ambushes you and steals your car - fight back and take him down.

  41. Rogue Altruists

    Baytree Canyon Road, Grand Senora Desert · After: Pulling Favors

    Take down Altruist Cult members assaulting a girl, then bring her home safe. Michael or Franklin only.

  42. Shop Robberies 1

    Eastbourne Way, Rockford Hills · After: Pulling Favors

    Run down the robbers fleeing a Bob Mulet barbershop, then keep or return what they stole.

  43. Shop Robberies 2

    Prosperity Street Promenade, Del Perro · After: Pulling Favors

    Chase a thief running from a SubUrban store robbery and choose to keep the cash or hand it back.

  44. Simeon Yetarian

    Adam's Apple Boulevard and Power Street, Strawberry · After: Friend Request · Do before: The Jewel Store Job

    Repel a group of enforcers Simeon Yetarian sends after you at the Premium Deluxe Motorsport lot. Michael or Franklin only.

  45. Snatched

    South Mo Milton Drive, Vinewood Hills · After: Pulling Favors

    Free a girl taken captive by Lost bikers and get her somewhere safe.

  46. Sport Bike Thief

    North Rockford Drive, Richman · After: Pulling Favors

    Run down someone making off with a sports bike, then return it to its owner or ride off with it yourself.

  47. Stag Do Running Man

    Route 68, Great Chaparral · After: Pulling Favors

    Untie a groom left bound to a post, then rush him to a bar and on to his wedding inside five game hours.

  48. Security Van 1

    La Puerta · After: Pulling Favors

    Take out the guard at the gas station before he stows the briefcase, snatch it, and shake the police.

  49. Security Van 2

    Little Seoul, near Vespucci Boulevard · After: Pulling Favors

    Eliminate the guard outside the Lucky Plucker before he gets the briefcase loaded, take it, and evade the cops.

  50. Security Van 3

    Paleto Bay · After: Pulling Favors

    Drop the guard stationed on Paleto Boulevard before he loads the case, grab it, and outrun the police.

  51. Security Van 4

    Little Seoul, near Adam's Apple Boulevard · After: Pulling Favors

    Take down the guard in a small lot before the briefcase gets loaded, then seize it and lose the police.

  52. Security Van 5

    Vespucci Canals, Prosperity Street · After: Pulling Favors

    Plant a sticky bomb on the rear doors of a moving security van, grab the case once it blows, and escape the cops.

  53. Security Van 6

    Little Seoul, San Andreas Avenue · After: Pulling Favors

    Blow the back doors of a van driving past Hit 'n' Run Coffee with a sticky bomb, take the briefcase, and lose the police.

  54. Security Van 7

    San Chianski Mountain Range, Chianski Passage · After: Pulling Favors

    Detonate a sticky bomb on a van's rear doors near the checkpoint guard, grab the case, and shake the cops.

  55. Security Van 8

    Tongva Hills, Great Ocean Highway · After: Pulling Favors

    Sticky-bomb the rear doors of a van rolling along Route 68, grab the briefcase inside, and get away from the police.

  56. Security Van 9

    Cypress Flats, El Rancho Boulevard · After: Pulling Favors

    Blast open a van's back doors near Orchardville Avenue with a sticky bomb, take the case, and outrun the cops.

  57. Security Van 10

    La Mesa, Supply Street · After: Pulling Favors

    Rig a sticky bomb to a van's back doors near Popular Street, seize the briefcase, and lose the police tail.
