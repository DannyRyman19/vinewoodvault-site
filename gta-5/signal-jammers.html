GTA Online Signal Jammers: all 50 locations

50 entries · updated 2026-10-20

GTA Online: 50 signal jammers hidden on rooftops and landmarks across San Andreas - destroy each for $2,000 + 1,000 RP. Clearing all 50 pays a $50,000 bonus and unlocks Avi Schwartzman as a hacker option for the Diamond Casino Heist. Added with the Diamond Casino Heist update.

Tick them off as you go. Vinewood Vault tracks all 50 Signal Jammers on an offline map, alongside everything else you need for 100% in GTA Online. Free on iPhone and iPad.
