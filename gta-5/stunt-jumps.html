GTA 5 Stunt Jumps: all 50 locations

50 entries · updated 2026-10-18

50 vehicle stunt jumps. Only 25 are required for the game's 100% stat.

Tick them off as you go. Vinewood Vault tracks all 50 Stunt Jumps on an offline map, alongside everything else you need for 100% in GTA 5. Free on iPhone and iPad.

  1. 1Stunt Jump 1

    Los Santos International Airport

    Same airport side, heading southwest against traffic: the ramp at the road's end by the tarmac entrance. Bike for the dodging.

    Los Santos International Airport
