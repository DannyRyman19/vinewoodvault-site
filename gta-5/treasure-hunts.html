GTA Online Treasure Hunts: all 3

3 entries · updated 2026-10-18

GTA Online one-time treasure hunts, each a short clue chain ending in a unique weapon, plus a bonus unlock for that weapon in Red Dead Redemption 2.

Tick them off as you go. Vinewood Vault tracks all 3 Treasure Hunts on an offline map, alongside everything else you need for 100% in GTA Online. Free on iPhone and iPad.

  1. Double Action Revolver

    Reward: GTA$250,000 and a Red Dead Redemption 2 unlock

    A mysterious photo arrives by email; follow it to the revolver. Landing 50 headshot kills with it pays GTA$250,000 and unlocks the weapon in Red Dead Redemption 2.

  2. Navy Revolver

    Reward: GTA$250,000 and a Red Dead Redemption 2 unlock

    Find the Los Santos Slasher's five clues and beat him for GTA$50,000 and the revolver. 50 kills with it pays the remaining GTA$200,000 and unlocks the weapon in Red Dead Redemption 2.

  3. Stone Hatchet

    Reward: GTA$250,000 and a Red Dead Redemption 2 unlock

    Clear five bounty targets for Maude to earn the hatchet's location. 25 rampage kills with it pay GTA$250,000 and unlock the weapon in Red Dead Redemption 2.
