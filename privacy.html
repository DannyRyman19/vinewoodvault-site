Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026‑08‑29

Draft. Review this and the Terms with a legal advisor before the App Store submission, and set the effective date.

Vinewood Vault does not collect, store, or transmit personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics.

What the app stores

Your collection progress (which items you have marked collected, your settings, and dashboard layout) is saved on your device only, in the app’s local storage and a shared container used by the app’s widgets. It is not sent anywhere and we cannot see it. Deleting the app removes it.

Purchases

Optional Premium features are sold through Apple’s in-app purchase system. Apple processes the payment and the subscription; the app only receives a yes/no entitlement status from Apple and never sees your payment details. Apple’s handling of that transaction is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Content loaded from the internet

The app loads collectible reference images from our content host and opens video walkthroughs on YouTube in your browser or the YouTube app. Making those requests reveals your device’s IP address to the host serving the file, as with any web request; we do not log or use that information to identify you. Once you open a YouTube link, YouTube’s own privacy policy applies.

Contacting us

If you use “Report an issue” in the app, it opens your email app with a pre-filled message that includes the app version and your device model — nothing is sent until you choose to send it.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
